# Platform Roster + Integrations Reference (PDF)

## What you'll get

A polished, shareable one-stop PDF listing every AI agent on the platform **plus** the third-party integrations, each with a plain-English job description — delivered to your Files.

## Contents (in order)

1. **Title page header** — "Aura Intercept — Platform Roster", dated Sep 20 2026.
2. **The 10 core operatives** — name + job (AI Receptionist, Customer Journey, Dispatch/GPS, Field Navigation, Business Finance, Admin, Outreach, Creative Content, Analytics Intelligence, Web Presence).
3. **The 24 legacy agent names** — grouped by the operative they route to (receptionist/emergency/intake/faq → AI Receptionist; booking/followup/review → Customer Journey; route/eta/checkin → Field Navigation; quoting/invoice/inventory/estimate/payments → Business Finance; campaign/lead/marketing → Outreach; insights/revenue/forecast/performance/analytics → Analytics Intelligence; creative/social_content/social_scheduler/social_analytics → Creative Content).
4. **The 14 industry specialists** — name + job, grouped by industry (field/repair, real estate, beauty & wellness, restaurants, personal assistant, universal).
5. **The 7 consoles** — C1 Customer Front Desk, C2 Field Ops/Dispatch, C3 Business Management, C4 Marketing & Outreach, C5 Social Media, C6 Analytics & Reports, C7 Reserved.
6. **NEW: Third-party integrations** — name + job for each connected service:
   - **Google Calendar** — two-way appointment scheduling sync
   - **SignalWire** — phone calls and SMS texting
   - **Resend** — transactional email delivery
   - **ElevenLabs** — AI voice for the receptionist / Talk to Aura
   - **Stripe** — payments, subscriptions and dunning
   - **Upload-Post** — one gateway publishing to all social platforms (billed by the customer)
   - **CRM / Lead Capture & Scoring** — lead intake and qualification
